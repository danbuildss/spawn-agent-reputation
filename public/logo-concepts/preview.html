Spawn Logo Concepts

Concept A: Connected Nodes

Central verified agent connected to network of other agents. Represents trust network and verification. Has checkmark in center.

Concept B: Trust Shield

Shield containing node network. Represents protection and security. More traditional "trust/security" feel.

Concept C: Spawn Burst

Nodes spawning outward from central source. Each node has verified indicator (white dot). Represents agents entering the ecosystem.

Concept D: Abstract S

Minimal S-shape formed by flowing nodes. Modern, clean, memorable. The "S" for Spawn.
